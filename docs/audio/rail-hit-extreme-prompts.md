## If Nothing Works: The Nuclear Option

Generate this completely different sound type, then use it as rail hit:

```
Low frequency room tone, brief bass rumble, soft air movement, heavily filtered to remove all highs, sounds like distant muffled movement, 120 milliseconds duration, no percussive quality
```

This won't sound exactly like a real rail hit, but it will at least be **soft and dampened** instead of bright and clacky.

---

## Test: Play It Next to Ball Collision

The best test is A/B comparison:

**Ball collision should sound:** Sharp, bright, clacky, percussive
**Rail hit should sound:** Soft, dull, muffled, cushioned

If they sound similar, the rail hit is **too bright** - try again with more extreme dampening descriptors.
