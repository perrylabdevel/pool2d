# Chest Asset Prompts

These prompts are designed to generate high-quality, game-ready assets for the "Chests" system. The style should match the existing "Vector Depth" aesthetic: clean, vibrant, semi-realistic but stylized, with strong lighting and depth.

## General Style Guidelines
- **Style**: Vector Depth / 3D Rendered Icon
- **Perspective**: Isometric or slightly top-down perspective (3/4 view).
- **Background**: Transparent (or solid color to be removed).
- **Lighting**: Soft, studio lighting with a clear key light to emphasize form. Glossy reflections.
- **Vibe**: Premium, rewarding, tactile.

---

## 1. Bronze Chest
**Concept**: A sturdy, reliable starter chest. Not fancy, but solid.
**Prompt**:
> A high-quality 3D game icon of a **Bronze Treasure Chest**. The chest is made of polished, slightly weathered bronze metal with iron reinforcements and rivets. It has a sturdy, simple iron padlock. The style is "Vector Depth" - clean lines, vibrant shading, and a glossy finish. Isometric view. The metal should look heavy and tactile. No background.

## 2. Gold Chest
**Concept**: A valuable, desirable reward. Shiny and rich.
**Prompt**:
> A high-quality 3D game icon of a **Gold Treasure Chest**. The chest is made of gleaming, polished gold with intricate engravings. It has a ruby red velvet lining visible at the seam. The lock is a shiny golden latch. The style is "Vector Depth" - highly reflective, warm golden tones, sparkling highlights. Isometric view. It should look expensive and heavy. No background.

## 3. Platinum Chest
**Concept**: A high-tier, futuristic or modern luxury chest. Sleek and cool.
**Prompt**:
> A high-quality 3D game icon of a **Platinum Treasure Chest**. The chest is made of sleek, brushed platinum or silver metal with neon blue light accents or glowing strips. It has a futuristic, seamless design. The style is "Vector Depth" - cool color palette (silver, white, cyan), sharp reflections, modern aesthetic. Isometric view. It should look high-tech and premium. No background.

## 4. Diamond Chest
**Concept**: The ultimate reward. Magical, translucent, and breathtaking.
**Prompt**:
> A high-quality 3D game icon of a **Diamond Treasure Chest**. The chest appears to be carved from a single block of translucent, glowing blue diamond or crystal. It refracts light with rainbow prisms. It has gold accents and a magical aura. The style is "Vector Depth" - ethereal, glowing, crystalline textures, sharp facets. Isometric view. It should look legendary and magical. No background.
