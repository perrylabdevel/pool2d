# AI Opponent Avatar Prompts

These prompts are designed to generate stylized character portraits for the AI opponents in RailRush. They follow a consistent mobile game art style.

## Global Style Settings
**Style:** Stylized 3D character portrait, premium mobile game art, vibrant colors, expressive facial features, smooth rendering, rim lighting, soft gradient background.
**Negative Prompt:** realistic, photo, noise, grain, text, watermark, logo, full body, messy, sketch, rough, low poly, pixelated, blurry.
**Aspect Ratio:** 1:1

---

## Bronze League

### Rookie Rick
**Bio:** Just started playing last week. Still figuring out which end of the cue to hold.
**Prompt:**
Stylized 3D character portrait of a young, confused-looking man named Rookie Rick. He is scratching his head and holding a pool cue upside down or awkwardly. He wears a simple t-shirt. Expression is bewildered but friendly. Warm lighting, soft orange/bronze background gradient. Mobile game art style, high quality, expressive.
