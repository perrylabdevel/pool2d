# Lobby Card Asset Prompts

These prompts are designed to generate high-quality, game-ready assets for the Lobby Cards. The style should match the existing "Vector Depth" aesthetic: clean, vibrant, semi-realistic but stylized, with strong lighting and depth.

## General Style Guidelines
- **Style**: Vector Depth / 3D Rendered Illustration
- **Perspective**: Front-facing or slightly angled dynamic composition.
- **Background**: Full card background, rich colors, subtle gradients or patterns.
- **Lighting**: Cinematic, inviting, glossy highlights.
- **Vibe**: Premium, exciting, engaging.
- **Aspect Ratio**: Approximately 1.5:1 (Landscape) or square-ish depending on usage, but prompts focus on content.

---

## 1. Shop Card
**Concept**: A marketplace for premium gear. Inviting, luxurious, and stocked.
**Prompt**:
> A high-quality 3D digital illustration for a mobile game "Shop" card. The image features a collection of premium billiards equipment: a sleek, polished custom pool cue with intricate inlay details, a cube of premium blue chalk, and a stack of gold coins or gems. The background is a rich, deep emerald green with a subtle diamond pattern. The lighting is warm and golden, highlighting the gloss on the cue and the sparkle of the currency. The style is "Vector Depth" - clean, vibrant, and expensive-looking. No text.

## 2. Events Card
**Concept**: A celebration of competition and victory. Exciting, dynamic, and rewarding.
**Prompt**:
> A high-quality 3D digital illustration for a mobile game "Events" card. The focal point is a gleaming gold trophy cup with a red ribbon. Behind it, there are dynamic elements like confetti, a tournament bracket graphic, or a spotlight beam. The background is a vibrant royal purple or deep blue with energy streaks. The lighting is dramatic, emphasizing the gold of the trophy. The style is "Vector Depth" - energetic, celebratory, and competitive. No text.
